What is box breathing?

Four equal sides, like a box.

Box breathing (sometimes called square breathing) is a simple, steady rhythm: you breathe in, hold, breathe out and hold again, each for the same count. Many people use it to slow down between meetings or reset before something stressful.

  1. 4Breathe in
  2. 4Hold
  3. 4Breathe out
  4. 4Hold

Breathe in through your nose if that's comfortable, and let the out-breath be slow and easy. If holding for four feels like too much, shorten the holds. There's no wrong way to take a calm minute.

If you feel dizzy or uncomfortable, stop and breathe normally. This is a relaxation exercise, not medical advice.
