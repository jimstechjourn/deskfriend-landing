Meet Pip

He sits on your toolbar and actually notices.

Tap Pip and he goes to sleep — reminders off, and the day doesn’t count against your streak, no guilt attached. Wake him whenever. Chrome has no way to tell “away from your desk” from “on a call, not touching the mouse,” so Pip errs kind: he still shows up, just without stealing focus, until you look over.

18 day streak — every day you hit target, Pip’s sprout grows a little more. Miss one and a freeze day covers you.

What’s inside

Built around how a desk day actually goes.

24 standing stretches

Every one of them gets you out of the chair — neck, shoulders, wrists, hips, calves. No seated stretch made the cut.

Guided box breathing

In 4, hold 4, out 4, hold 4. For the meetings you can’t stand up out of.

Idle-aware

Quiet when you’ve stepped away, gentle (not focus-stealing) when you’re just on a call.

Streaks with freeze days

One off day doesn’t end a streak — you earn a freeze back every 7-day run.

Put Pip to sleep, anytime

One tap silences reminders for as long as you need — the day just goes neutral, no penalty.

Configurable interval

Every 15, 30, 45, or 60 minutes — and 1 to 3 stretches per prompt.

Privacy

Your streak lives in your browser. Not on our servers — we don’t have any.

Everything stays right there on your machine. There’s no account to create, nothing syncs to a company, and nothing about how you use it is collected, ever.
